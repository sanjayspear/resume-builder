# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Resume Builder to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone,
whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing someone else's private information (such as an address or a
  resume) without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, report it to the maintainer,
Sanjay Y M ([@sanjayspear](https://github.com/sanjayspear) on GitHub), using
the contact details on that GitHub profile. You can also use GitHub's
**Report content** option on the comment or issue. Reports are handled
promptly and confidentially.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues
that break this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour is harmful.
